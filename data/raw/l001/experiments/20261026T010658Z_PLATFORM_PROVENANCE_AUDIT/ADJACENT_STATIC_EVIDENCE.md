## Confirmed issues, not platform speculation

1. Historical floating knot selection changes polynomial pieces with precision. The deterministic integer-selection successor fixes the representation contract; the original raw-coefficient gate remains failed. This is an identified representation issue, not an observed Mac-versus-Linux disagreement.
2. P01 whole-flow historical authentication is unresolved by the bounded Oct13 search. A checksum inside an unauthenticated enclosing result does not authenticate that result. The trace predicate check is genuine but narrower than a flow proof.
3. C6 preparation failed the prescribed inclusion estimate despite a small derivative bound. Increasing arithmetic precision did not establish existence. Linux production does not convert this adverse result into success.

## Runtime and scope boundaries

The inspected proof-bearing family uses Python/Arb/Fraction and arbitrary-precision arithmetic; this inspection found no basis to assert a C++ `long double` or FMA porting defect in the current certificates. A targeted text search is not proof that no such dependency exists anywhere. Native-library identity and rounding assumptions remain part of the foundations/recent-AB assessment.

No conclusion here requires rerunning every historical L001 packet. Reproduction or stronger certification of an adjacent claim should be requested for that claim, with its own frozen gates. Historical GH support, causal-window diagnostics and horizon work are not validated by this static audit and are not proposed for rerun as part of the current AB manuscript dependency closure.
