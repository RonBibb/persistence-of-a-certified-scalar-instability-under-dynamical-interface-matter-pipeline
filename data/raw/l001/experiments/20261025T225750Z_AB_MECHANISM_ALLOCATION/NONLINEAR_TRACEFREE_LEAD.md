# Prospective second-order tracefree metric lead

Analytical scoping only, from the actual manuscript S6 first row; not an executed second-order calculation.

Write p=z p1+z² p2+..., b=z b1+z² b2+..., f=z f1+..., with z=delta² exp(-6 h t). The accepted first-current construction has c1=b1+2p1=0 identically. Thus graph transport acting on c1 contributes nothing at order z². Background time derivatives vanish in the pulled formulation.

At order z² the explicit quadratic source in S6 is

    (Db)^2 - 2(Dp)(Db) - 2(Dp)^2 + (Df)^2
      = 36 k z² (6 p1² + f1²).

The new coefficient c2=b2+2p2 has rate r2=-12h, so its linear row is r2(r2-h)c2=156 k c2. Consequently

    c2 = -(3/13)(6 p1²+f1²).

For real coefficients this is nonpositive, and strictly negative wherever the first-current response is nonzero. For both currents together, p1 and f1 are their summed first-order profiles before squaring; the mixed-current term cannot be discarded.

Meaning: the first-order relation b=-2p necessarily acquires a nonlinear correction in this diagonal/pulled chart. This is a required term of the known field equations, not evidence of missing physics. c2 alone is not a gauge-invariant proper-separation or radar coefficient and does not supply p2, f2, second-order interface graphs, oscillator response, or a finite-charge existence radius. A meaningful n=2 branch requires all compatible S6/S7/Q5 sources and their boundary reconstruction, not simply a smaller residual at chosen amplitudes.
