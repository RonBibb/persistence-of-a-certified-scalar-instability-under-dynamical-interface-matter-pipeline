# Selected neutral material sensitivities

This prospective calculation concerns the selected neutral AB action and its simple negative scalar mode, not a charged spectrum or the separate concave example. It depends on accepted T2 selected-action endpoint and norm enclosures. The centered oscillator parameters can change without changing the neutral background because the interaction and its first derivatives vanish at that background.

For each wall let A_i=a_i², w_i=omega_i², d_i=w_i+beta_i-mu/A_i, and

Gamma_i=kappa_i+beta_i eta_i²-beta_i² eta_i²/d_i.

The negative sector has d_i>0. At fixed background and fixed mu,

- Gamma_kappa=1.
- Gamma_beta=eta²(w-mu/A)²/d².
- Gamma_w=beta² eta²/d².
- Gamma_eta=2 beta eta(1-beta/d).
- Gamma_mu=−beta² eta²/(A d²).

The original-action constant-Hessian Green identity gives alpha_Gamma_i=a_i^4 f_i²/Z_A at a normalized eigenmode. Differentiating the selected fixed point mu=alpha(Gamma_a(mu,p),Gamma_b(mu,p)) yields

mu_p = [sum_i a_i^4 f_i² Gamma_{i,p}]/[Z_A−sum_i a_i^4 f_i² Gamma_{i,mu}].

The denominator is exactly Z_ext=Z_A+sum_i a_i² u_i² because u_i=beta_i eta_i f_i/d_i. Thus a one-wall parameter derivative is

mu_p_i=a_i^4 f_i² Gamma_{i,p}/Z_ext.

These are local eigenvalue susceptibilities with the centered background fixed. They are not energy fractions, finite-amplitude stabilization thresholds, or a partition of causal responsibility for the instability. The parameter w is omega squared, not omega.
