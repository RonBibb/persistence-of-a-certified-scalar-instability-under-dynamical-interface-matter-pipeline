# Uniform contraction and identification of the inherited local branch

Fix the exact D1 background and a physical normalized mode admitted by the
unchanged certificates. Their enclosing rectangles are uncertainty bounds,
not an assertion that every arbitrary point satisfies the mode identities.
Let G(e,x) be the root-equivalent original coupled boundary map. It is real
analytic wherever the certified IVPs, exponentials and wall preparation are
defined. Let T_e(x)=x-CG(e,x), with nonsingular exact C.

If one tile I admits the uniform self-map and contraction gates on the closed
complete weighted box X, Banach's theorem gives a unique x(e) in X at EVERY
e in I. This does not follow from finitely many point solutions: the saved
center and derivative evaluations include the entire interval parameter.
The norm bound <1 implies G_x is invertible everywhere in I x X. Continuity
in e follows from the uniform contraction estimate, and local analyticity
follows from the analytic implicit-function theorem about each root. The
strict interior margin also supplies a local open neighborhood at endpoints.

Two admitted tiles use the same X. Their roots at an overlap or shared endpoint
are equal by uniqueness in X; consequently a gap-free finite closed cover is
one connected branch. Disjoint covered components are not joined by assertion.

The certified local family has x(0)=0 and x'(0)=0 in the shifted chart. Its
values lie in X on a sufficiently small neighborhood by continuity. Hence
the uniform branch agrees there by uniqueness; exact G_e(0,0)=0 retains the
original physical tangent. This argument needs no extrapolation from the
tiny explicit local radius and supplies no spacetime evolution theorem.

An old endpoint root satisfies x_old in center_old+image_offset_old. The
independent audit re-forms this image from the old G/J and rational arithmetic.
If this image is inside the new X, that old root is also the unique new
endpoint root. Overlap of boxes alone would not prove this; image containment
is required. Both endpoints must pass this test for the full advertised claim.

For epsilon Taylor coefficients a_n of C7 and W, the degree-seven polynomial
plus a_8(hull(0,I))*I^8 encloses the exact functions uniformly in I by Taylor's
theorem applied separately at each e. The derivative interval must cover the
segment from0 to EVERY e, including one-sided tiles. Its shifted-center
coefficient construction retains all terms. Only a_0=a_1=0 at the original
zero center use the already independently derived physical identities.
