# Action priority after a compatible companion exists

## Parallel work packages

### P0 — immutable companion admission

Bind the repaired source, action, initial/boundary data, runtime, restart and
simultaneous bulk-wall residual result.  This is the sole predecessor gate.
Nothing below may reinterpret an incomplete P0 checkpoint as admission.
One admitted resolution permits pilot measurements.  Claim-grade causal use
waits for the separately bound evolution-resolution and outer-domain controls.

### P1 — short geometric persistence test

On a short, predeclared interval, determine whether the outer MOTS lineage,
spacelike signature, area growth and positive finite-cylinder margins survive
the compatibility repair at two evolution resolutions.  This answers whether
the represented P407 geometry was robust to the physical repair.  It is not an
event-horizon test.

Use the predecessor's physical radii \(R=4,6,8\) as fixed comparability
controls and retain \(R|\mathcal E|/2\) plus every term in \(\mathcal E\).
Their old positive margins are not acceptance thresholds for the repaired
model and may not be copied into its error budget.

### P2 — portable terminal-front lane

Build four exact terminal interfaces and run the coordinate/Hamiltonian null
families plus method, surface, evolution and domain transfers.  Retain `b`,
`lambda` and the `B_t` tail diagnostic.  This can run read-only in parallel by
sealed terminal block.

### P3 — exterior and capture lane

Specify \(E\), prove or bound \(W\subset E\), and audit the finite-barrier
hypotheses on the repaired model.  Numerical cylinder surveys select candidate
radii only.  The all-future sign and continuum cap qualification are analytic
or validated-numerics obligations.

### P4 — opposite-witness bracket

Use the strict launch-fate interface with one visible and one captured event on
each predeclared launch curve.  Refine nested physical-length brackets.  One
pair proves one intersection; a cross-section needs a continuous, noncrossing
atlas covering all labels and both rims.  Do not use a role-dependent event ID
or later ray entry as launch capture.

### P5 — identification

Compare the P2 front uncertainty tube with the P4 bracket on common slices and
one held-out terminal quartet.  This is the first stage that may describe a
terminal front as a locator of the end-relative horizon.

## Scheduling consequence

P2 and P3/P4 should not be serialized.  Once P0 seals a terminal block, P2 can
consume it while P3 continues independently.  P1 is short and determines
whether further terminals are worth producing.  P5 alone waits for both lanes.
This prevents another build-run-wait cycle from consuming an entire research
window before the logically independent exterior question is addressed.
