# Viability assessment

## What improved

The causal line is more viable than it was at the end of O4 because the work is
no longer serialized behind terminal-MOTS settling.  Horizon existence and
null-sheet localization are now separate lanes with a precise join condition.
A certified opposite-fate bracket remains scientifically useful even if the
MOTS-seeded sheets repeat O2's nonlocalization.

Most numerical machinery needed for the locator lane already exists and has
positive controls: the O1 atomic interface pattern, the O2 dual-formulation
tracer/comparator, the 12-case stationary black-string control, and the strict
launch-fate logic.  The required work is parameterization and new physical
bindings, not a clean-room rewrite.

## What remains difficult

The dominant gap is not null integration.  It is supplying one genuine
same-development all-future exterior/worldtube authority and a continuum-level
captured endpoint.  A finite compatible simulation can measure favorable
margins and reject bad constructions, but cannot by duration alone prove their
all-future persistence.

That analytic target is now concrete: preserve
\(\theta^+/k^r=2/R+\mathcal E>0\) on the barrier, including the compact-tilt,
warp-slope and time-derivative terms, and prove the required causal relation
between its immediately outer end and the program's declared exterior.

The next short companion is therefore a high-value branching test:

- if simultaneous bulk-wall compatibility destroys the outer MOTS or the
  favorable cylinder margins, stop the present causal construction and repair
  the physical model before spending on long traces;
- if they survive at two resolutions, run the locator and exterior lanes in
  parallel; and
- if a causal bracket succeeds while the terminal family does not, retain the
  bracket and replace the estimator rather than declaring the line a failure.

## Bottom line

The line is viable but not close to an event-horizon claim.  It now has a
falsifiable and compute-efficient architecture.  The decisive risk is whether
the physical exterior/barrier hypotheses can be supplied for the repaired
model, not whether another long P407-like evolution can be produced.
