# Remaining issues and ranked next steps

## Current decision

The static line has crossed the threshold for a narrow theorem manuscript. It
has not crossed the threshold for submission. The distinction is important:
the remaining work is primarily article construction, archive packaging, and
bounded novelty review—not another search for the same root.

## Live issues

### 1. The public package does not yet exist

The source-tree evidence passes on macOS and Linux, but the actual compact
archive has not been staged and replayed. Current producers retain research
tree imports. In particular, the D1 high-resolution wrapper imports its base
producer by module name, and D2A authenticates four relative parent files. A
copying or path error could therefore make an otherwise correct paper package
non-reproducible.

**Consequence:** manuscript drafting may proceed; submission should wait for a
clean relative-path archive replay.

### 2. Novelty is bounded, not globally established

The focused primary-source chain found the ingredients separately and did not
find the exact paired certificate. It also found prior work anticipating local
uniqueness for generic curved two-wall problems.

**Consequence:** claim the model-specific paired theorem “to our knowledge.”
Do not claim that curved scalar branes, scalar-selected spacing, local
uniqueness, or computer-assisted Einstein existence are new.

### 3. The parameter result is source-specific

The theorem fixes exact binary64 literals and one launch interval. It does not
prove persistence under parameter perturbation. The lower tension is
reconstructed from the unchanged source constructor rather than independently
archived in the older source record.

**Consequence:** make source-pinned compatibility the motivation. Do not use
“generic,” “robust in parameter space,” or “universal.”

### 4. The two certificate scopes remain narrow

D1 uniqueness is local to the frozen root interval and first-event branch. D2A
excludes positive-curvature roots only at exact `D0` and on exact
`0<=a<=v0`. It does not exclude launches outside that interval,
zero/negative-curvature roots, altered wall potentials, or less symmetric
geometries.

**Consequence:** the corollary is a certified mismatch in the declared reduced
family, not a global classification of the action.

### 5. Stability and dynamics are not inherited

The static root's source ancestry does not make it a state on the old collapse
lineage. Its separation differs, and a flat de Sitter slicing has nonzero
extrinsic curvature. No admitted Cauchy bridge exists.

**Consequence:** keep stability, radion motion, collapse, MOTSs, event horizons,
and baby universes out of the theorem paper's claims. The prospective causal
contract remains a separate research program.

### 6. One benign enclosure diagnostic is non-monotone

The independent curvature interval is strictly positive at both step levels,
but its width increases by a factor about 1.0000297 from 8,192 to 16,384 steps.
Exact interval-Newton nesting, not monotone contraction of every derived
quantity, was the frozen gate.

**Consequence:** preserve this diagnostic in the supplement and avoid claiming
uniform monotone refinement of all enclosures. It does not threaten the sign of
the certified curvature.

## Ranked next steps

### Priority 1 — write the narrow article

Use `MANUSCRIPT_BLUEPRINT.md` and `CLAIM_TO_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md`. Lead with the
paired compatibility result and its exact separation contrast. Put the proof
inventory, hashes, and protocol history outside the main narrative.

**Estimated effort:** one focused drafting session for a complete first article
draft, followed by a separate equation/reference audit.

### Priority 2 — build and replay the compact proof archive

Copy the Tier-2 files in `PUBLIC_ARCHIVE_INVENTORY.md` into a new staging tree,
flatten or prospectively adapt imports, regenerate the manifest, and run the
commands in `REPRODUCIBILITY_COMMAND_MAP.md` in the frozen Linux environment.
Then run certificate-only verification on macOS and Linux.

**Acceptance:** all source hashes accounted for, both D1 results meet their
frozen gates, D2A recreates the exact cover with zero unresolved boxes, and the
staged relative-path verifier passes.

### Priority 3 — complete conventional novelty review

Use forward/backward citation databases and the closest papers' bibliographies
to test the exact paired claim. Record search strings, dates, and dispositions.
This is a literature task, not a reason to delay drafting the mathematical
core.

### Priority 4 — independent manuscript-level proof review

Give a reviewer the action, reduction, theorem statement, exact authority
table, and clean archive without the protocol narrative. Ask specifically
about junction conventions, interval-Newton uniqueness language, completeness
of the D2A cover, and whether the novelty sentence is appropriately bounded.

### Priority 5 — only then consider scientific extensions

Possible extensions include a prospectively fixed parameter neighborhood,
stability of the certified static branch, or the Cauchy bridge specified by the
future causal contract. None is required for the narrow theorem to stand, and
none should be inserted into the current paper unless it independently closes
its own gates.

## Viability

The theorem line is viable. Its strongest advantage is that its truth no longer
depends on the unresolved collapse and causal programs. Its principal risk is
whether a referee finds one source-pinned parameter point significant enough,
because the qualitative phase-space, shooting, multiplicity, and dynamics
picture already exists in prior work. Presentation is therefore substantive:
if framed as a failed black-hole or baby-universe construction, the actual
mathematical result disappears; if framed as a paired validated
existence/exclusion theorem answering a concrete compatibility question, the
result is coherent, auditable, and appropriately bounded.
