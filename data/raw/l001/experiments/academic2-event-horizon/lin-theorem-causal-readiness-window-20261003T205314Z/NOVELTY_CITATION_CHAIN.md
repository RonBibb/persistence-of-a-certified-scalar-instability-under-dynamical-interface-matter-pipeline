# Focused novelty and citation chain for the Lin static theorem

## Question searched

The search question is intentionally narrower than “are bulk-scalar branes
known?”:

> Has the literature already proved, for one fixed two-wall Einstein--AdS--scalar
> action and fixed wall parameters, the paired statement consisting of (i) a
> validated unique positive-curvature solution when the second wall is placed at
> the first transverse Israel event and (ii) an exhaustive exclusion of a
> positive-curvature solution at a separately prescribed proper separation over
> the full declared launch interval?

Searches on 2026-10-03 combined the terms `two brane`, `bulk scalar`, `curved
brane`, `shooting`, `interval arithmetic`, `interval Newton`, `rigorous
numerics`, `computer-assisted proof`, `Einstein scalar`, and `boundary value
problem`.  The chain below uses primary papers and official arXiv records.  A
negative search is not a priority proof; the conclusion is bounded to the
sources and query families recorded here.

## Backward citation chain: physical model class

1. **Goldberger and Wise, “Modulus Stabilization with Bulk Fields,”**
   [arXiv:hep-ph/9907447](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9907447), Phys. Rev.
   Lett. 83 (1999) 4922--4925,
   [doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.4922](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.4922).
   This establishes the bulk-scalar mechanism for
   stabilizing the Randall--Sundrum modulus using interactions localized on two
   branes.  Therefore bulk-scalar stabilization and a scalar-selected brane
   spacing are not novel claims of the present work.

2. **DeWolfe, Freedman, Gubser, and Karch, “Modeling the fifth dimension with
   scalars and gravity,”** [arXiv:hep-th/9909134](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9909134),
   Phys. Rev. D 62 (2000) 046008,
   [doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.62.046008](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.62.046008).
   They give a nonlinear scalar--gravity
   treatment, state that inter-brane spacing is uniquely determined after the
   flat-brane fine tuning, and discuss curved de Sitter/anti-de Sitter solutions
   when that tuning is imperfect.  This is the closest conceptual antecedent
   for treating separation and four-dimensional curvature as jointly selected.
   Their result does not, from the material inspected here, supply the present
   model-specific interval enclosure or the fixed-distance exhaustive cover.

   Full-text check: their parameter count says a curved problem should
   generically have a locally unique solution, and their analytic
   cosmological-constant example has a unique solution after its parameters
   are fixed.  The present article therefore must **not** claim that local
   uniqueness of a curved two-brane solution is new.  Its narrower contribution
   is validating the source-pinned massive-scalar/quadratic-wall branch and
   pairing that result with complete exclusion at a second prescribed
   separation.

3. **Kanti, Olive, and Pospelov, “Static Solutions for Brane Models with a Bulk
   Scalar Field,”** [arXiv:hep-ph/0002229](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0002229),
   Phys. Lett. B 481 (2000) 386--396,
   [doi:10.1016/S0370-2693(00)00465-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0370-2693(00)00465-2).
   They construct exact static solutions and show
   that scalar--brane interactions determine the inter-brane distance, while
   exposing correlations among bulk cosmological constant, brane energies, and
   scalar couplings.  This further removes static scalar-selected distance as a
   novelty claim.

   Their discussion also warns that fixed distance can arise from imposing a
   time-independent, overdetermined system and can require correlations among
   wall and bulk parameters.  This is directly relevant to D2A: the exclusion
   is a static compatibility theorem, not evidence for a dynamical force moving
   the walls.

4. **Lesgourgues and Sorbo, “Goldberger-Wise variations: stabilizing brane
   models with a bulk scalar,”** [arXiv:hep-th/0310007](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0310007),
   Phys. Rev. D 69 (2004) 084010,
   [doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.69.084010](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.69.084010).
   They treat generic one- and two-brane models
   with arbitrary bulk and brane scalar potentials and derive stability
   criteria.  The present theorem must not be framed as a general stabilization
   theorem or a stability analysis.

## Forward and neighboring chain: curved scalar branes

5. **Pal and Kar, “de Sitter branes with a bulk scalar,”**
   [arXiv:hep-th/0701266](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0701266), Gen. Relativ.
   Gravit. 41 (2009) 1165--1179,
   [doi:10.1007/s10714-008-0697-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10714-008-0697-2).
   They exhibit exactly solvable warped five-dimensional
   scalar models with de Sitter or anti-de Sitter induced geometry, including a
   thin two-brane case.  Curved scalar branes themselves are therefore not the
   contribution.

6. **Ghosh, Kiritsis, Nitti, and Witkowski, “De Sitter and Anti-de Sitter branes
   in self-tuning models,”** [arXiv:1807.09794](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.09794),
   JHEP 11 (2018) 128,
   [doi:10.1007/JHEP11(2018)128](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP11(2018)128).
   They analyze existence and nonexistence of maximally
   symmetric curved branes in a different dilatonic self-tuning framework.  The
   comparison is useful because it shows that existence can depend sharply on
   asymptotics and source data, but it is not the same action or boundary-value
   theorem.

## Closest phase-space, shooting, and dynamics comparators

7. **Felder, Frolov, and Kofman, “Warped Geometry of Brane Worlds,”**
   [arXiv:hep-th/0112165](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0112165), Class. Quantum
   Grav. 19 (2002) 2983--3002,
   [doi:10.1088/0264-9381/19/11/315](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/19/11/315).
   They study the same broad mathematical architecture:
   a five-dimensional warp factor and bulk scalar with constant-curvature
   slices, describe curved solutions through a three-dimensional phase
   portrait, and represent a brane junction condition as a one-dimensional
   curve in that phase space. This removes phase-space shooting and wall-curve
   intersection as novelty claims. It also supplies the closest conceptual
   language for explaining why specified wall potentials need not admit a
   compatible second-wall placement.

8. **Martin, Felder, Frolov, Peloso, and Kofman, “Braneworld dynamics with the
   BraneCode,”** [arXiv:hep-th/0309001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0309001),
   Phys. Rev. D 69 (2004) 084017,
   [doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.69.084017](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.69.084017).
   They numerically solve time-dependent
   five-dimensional two-brane scalar systems, use shooting to construct static
   configurations for specified brane potentials, report that such boundary
   problems may have no solution or finitely many solutions, and study
   instability-driven evolution between warped configurations. This is a
   particularly important boundary: neither numerical shooting, multiplicity
   of warped solutions, nor dynamical restructuring between them is new here.
   The current theorem contributes rigorous, source-pinned existence and
   exclusion certificates; it does not inherit their dynamical conclusions.

## Method chain: computer-assisted general relativity and Einstein geometry

9. **Reiterer and Trubowitz, “Choptuik's critical spacetime exists,”**
   [arXiv:1203.3766](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.3766), Commun. Math. Phys. 368
   (2019) 143--186,
   [doi:10.1007/s00220-019-03413-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-019-03413-8).
   They use rigorous error bounds and computer assistance to turn an
   approximate Einstein--scalar solution into an existence proof.  Hence
   “computer-assisted proof in general relativity” is not a novelty claim.

10. **Buttsworth and Hodgkinson, “Computationally-assisted proof of a novel
   O(3) x O(10)-invariant Einstein metric on S12,”**
   [arXiv:2412.01184](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01184) (2024).  They combine a
   high-precision approximate cohomogeneity-one metric with rigorous analysis
   to prove a genuine Einstein metric exists.  This is a recent methodological
   comparator, not a physical-model antecedent.

11. **Wang, “Computer-assisted construction of SU(2)-invariant negative Einstein
   metrics,”** [arXiv:2504.21644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21644), Ann. Global
   Anal. Geom. 69 (2026), Paper 18,
   [doi:10.1007/s10455-026-10037-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10455-026-10037-4).
   This work uses rigorous numerics and a
   fixed-point argument to construct complete negative Einstein metrics.  It
   reinforces that rigorous numerics is an accepted proof ingredient while
   remaining geometrically distinct from the two-wall Lorentzian model.

## Bounded novelty assessment

The search **did locate** the ingredients separately:

- stabilized two-brane scalar models;
- scalar-selected inter-brane distance;
- curved de Sitter/anti-de Sitter branes;
- phase-space wall curves, numerical shooting, multiplicity, and exact static
  solutions;
- nonlinear dynamics and instability-driven restructuring of curved two-brane
  scalar configurations;
- computer-assisted existence proofs for Einstein and Einstein--scalar systems.

The search **did not locate** the exact paired theorem proved by the sealed D1
and D2A records: a validated unique root on the narrow first-event branch and a
complete interval cover excluding a positive-curvature solution at the fixed
production separation, both under the same fixed action, wall potentials, and
binary64 parameter literals.  That pairing is the defensible candidate
contribution.  It should be described as “to our knowledge” until a conventional
database/citation review is completed, not as an absolute first.

The full-text stress test makes the boundary sharper. Prior work already
anticipates local uniqueness for generic curved boundary-value problems,
represents junction conditions as phase-space curves, numerically shoots
specified two-brane potentials, finds multiple warped configurations, and
evolves instabilities between them. Accordingly, the publishable distinction
is not “a curved branch exists” or “one wall placement fails.” It is the
machine-checkable conjunction, for the exact source-pinned parameter literals,
of a validated first-event root with independently replayed geometry and an
exhaustive no-root cover at the original proper separation.

The scientific inference is narrower than a new stabilization mechanism.  For
the declared parameters, the field equations and junction conditions select a
regular curved branch only after the upper-wall position is allowed to move,
whereas the prescribed production separation is rigorously excluded over the
declared launch interval.  This demonstrates a model-specific incompatibility
between fixed separation and the certified positive-curvature static branch.

## Claims the literature search does not license

- global uniqueness outside the certified launch interval;
- nonlinear or spectral stability of the D1 branch;
- dynamical selection of D1 from the collapse data;
- collapse, trapped-surface, event-horizon, or baby-universe conclusions;
- a universal theorem for arbitrary wall potentials or bulk scalar potentials;
- an absolute priority claim.

## Recommended manuscript placement

Use Goldberger--Wise and DeWolfe et al. in the opening model paragraph; Kanti et
al. and Lesgourgues--Sorbo when distinguishing parameter selection from
stability; Pal--Kar and Ghosh et al. in the curved-brane context paragraph;
Felder--Frolov--Kofman and Martin et al. for the closest phase-space, shooting,
and dynamical antecedents; and one or two of Reiterer--Trubowitz,
Buttsworth--Hodgkinson, and Wang in the computer-assisted-method paragraph. The
main text should present the paired certificate as the contribution and move
the complete verification inventory to the supplement or archive.
