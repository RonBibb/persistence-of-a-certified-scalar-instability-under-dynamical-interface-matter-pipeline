# Focused primary-literature novelty refresh

## Search question

The search asked whether a prior paper already supplies the exact conjunction
now available here: a computer-assisted global uniqueness certificate for a
positive-curvature first-event two-wall Einstein--scalar solution, proof that
the solution is isolated in `(a,D)`, and an exhaustive positive-curvature
no-root cover at a second prescribed separation for the same fixed action and
wall data.

The October 5, 2026 search used combinations of `two brane`, `bulk scalar`,
`curved brane`, `first event`, `shooting`, `interval Newton`, `interval
arithmetic`, `computer-assisted proof`, `existence`, and `nonexistence`, and
checked primary arXiv records and publisher metadata. A negative search is not
a priority proof.

## Closest physical antecedents

- Goldberger and Wise established bulk-scalar modulus stabilization
  ([arXiv:hep-ph/9907447](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9907447)). Scalar-selected
  wall spacing is therefore not novel.
- DeWolfe, Freedman, Gubser, and Karch developed nonlinear scalar--gravity
  wall solutions and discussed curved branches
  ([arXiv:hep-th/9909134](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9909134)). Curved
  scalar-supported walls and generic local selection are not novel.
- Felder, Frolov, and Kofman described constant-curvature scalar-brane
  solutions through phase-space trajectories and wall curves
  ([arXiv:hep-th/0112165](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0112165)). Shooting and
  wall-curve intersection are not novel.
- Martin, Felder, Frolov, Peloso, and Kofman numerically constructed and
  evolved two-brane scalar configurations, including cases with no static
  solution, finitely many solutions, and tachyonic restructuring
  ([arXiv:hep-th/0309001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0309001)). Neither
  numerical shooting, multiplicity/nonexistence, nor dynamical transitions are
  novelty claims here. Their stability results also show why the current short
  GH response must not be called stability.
- Lesgourgues and Sorbo derived stability criteria for broad one- and two-brane
  scalar models ([arXiv:hep-th/0310007](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0310007)).
  The present static theorem is not a general stabilization theorem.

## Computer-assisted gravity antecedents

- Reiterer and Trubowitz used rigorous error control to prove existence of an
  Einstein--scalar critical spacetime
  ([arXiv:1203.3766](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.3766)).
- Buttsworth and Hodgkinson used computer assistance to prove an invariant
  Einstein metric ([arXiv:2412.01184](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01184)).
- Wang constructed negative Einstein metrics with computer assistance
  ([arXiv:2504.21644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21644)).

Computer-assisted existence in Einstein geometry is therefore an established
methodological class, not the contribution.

## Bounded novelty judgment

No exact antecedent was located for the upgraded paired theorem under one
fixed two-wall model:

1. exactly one positive-curvature first-event simultaneous-wall solution on
   the full declared launch interval;
2. an independently replayed geometric enclosure for that solution;
3. proof that the solution is an isolated zero of the two wall residuals; and
4. exhaustive exclusion at a separately prescribed separation with a uniform
   residual floor.

The defensible candidate novelty is this **model-specific certificate
architecture and compatibility conclusion**, not any individual physical or
numerical ingredient. Submission prose should say “to our knowledge” and
retain the fixed-parameter and reduced-ansatz qualifications.

The remaining literature task is conventional forward/backward citation
chaining through the two closest physical papers and database searches in
INSPIRE, MathSciNet, and zbMATH. The present web search does not replace that
review.
