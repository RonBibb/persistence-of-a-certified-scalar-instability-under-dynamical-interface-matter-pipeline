# Manuscript claim package

## Recommended title

**A certified isolated separation in a five-dimensional Einstein--scalar
two-wall model**

More descriptive alternative:

**Computer-assisted global uniqueness and fixed-separation exclusion in a
five-dimensional Einstein--scalar two-wall problem**

## Abstract seed

We prove a paired computer-assisted theorem for a fixed five-dimensional
Einstein--scalar model on a two-wall interval. Reduction of the warped,
maximally symmetric field equations and junction conditions gives a
first-event shooting problem in the lower-wall scalar value. Validated interval
integration, interval Newton, and a gap-free dual-producer cover establish
exactly one positive-curvature simultaneous-wall solution over the complete
declared launch interval. The solution has proper separation near `1.9975`,
and an independent Taylor--Picard implementation encloses its curvature, warp,
conformal length, first integral, and wall identities. The full Jacobian of the
two wall residuals is bounded away from zero, so the solution is isolated rather
than a continuous fixed-model branch. At the separately prescribed separation
`D0=0.9996389747145851`, an exact 693-box cover excludes every admissible
positive-curvature solution and gives a uniform residual-vector floor above
`0.0077`. Thus the separation mismatch is a certified compatibility property
of this reduced static family, not a missed numerical root. The theorem does
not establish stability, dynamical selection, collapse, or a causal horizon.

## Headline inference

The desired curved static branch exists, but for the fixed source-pinned wall
model it exists at one isolated selected separation and not at the originally
prescribed separation.

## Main-paper argument

1. **Question.** Can one fixed two-wall Einstein--scalar model simultaneously
   realize positive four-curvature and the prescribed wall separation?
2. **Reduction.** Derive the proper-distance IVP, first integral, wall launch,
   first-event residual `q(a)`, and fixed-distance residual vector `F(a,D)`.
3. **Existence and global uniqueness.** Present the strict interval-Newton
   certificate, the independent interval implementation, and the exact
   full-launch cover with one root box.
4. **Isolation.** Present the nonzero two-by-two residual Jacobian and explain
   why `D` is selected, not a free continuation coordinate in the unchanged
   model.
5. **Exclusion at `D0`.** Present the exact 693-box cover and `0.0077` residual
   floor.
6. **Interpretation.** The fixed inputs overconstrain the desired branch at
   `D0`; allowing the upper wall to be selected yields one compatible curved
   configuration near twice that separation.
7. **Boundary.** Parameter robustness, stability, dynamics, collapse, and
   horizon claims remain open and separate.
