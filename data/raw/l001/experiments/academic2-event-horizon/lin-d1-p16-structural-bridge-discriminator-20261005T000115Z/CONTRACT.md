# D1-P16 structural bridge discriminator

## Question

Decide whether the already sealed Lin D1 and P16 lineages are related by an
invertible coordinate/orientation/field normalization, a gauge or source
translation, or an already-derived controlled family.  Preserve the failed
direct comparison and do not alter either endpoint.

## Bound evidence and fixed rules

- Rehash every source listed in `verify_obstruction.py` before evaluation.
- Treat D1's action literals and P16's archived background literals exactly as
  serialized.  Do not round one into the other.
- Treat wall-to-wall proper length and the range/nonconstancy of a scalar field
  as diffeomorphism invariants.  Wall orientation may exchange endpoints but
  cannot change the length.  An invertible field normalization cannot map a
  nonconstant scalar to the identically zero field.
- Preserve the D1 theorem's `chi=0`, the P16 array, the 32/72 comparison, all
  gates, and the D1/P16 sources.
- Use the certified D1 Jacobian determinant and D2A cover only within their
  fixed reduced static action and launch domain.
- A gauge/source-reference difference is not a physical obstruction.
- A missing admitted P16 endpoint or absent deformation family is a missing
  prerequisite, not a theorem of universal nonexistence.

## Classification rule

A positive bridge requires either an invariant-preserving equivalence of the
two sealed endpoints or an already-derived family of complete solutions whose
endpoints are those objects.  Otherwise return a scoped no-join and identify
the smallest new test that does not retune either endpoint.
