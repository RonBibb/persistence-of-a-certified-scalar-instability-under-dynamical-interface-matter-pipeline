# Manuscript viability decision

## Verdict: CONDITIONAL GO

There is a genuinely publishable mathematical core if the paper is framed as
a narrow computer-assisted theorem for a fixed five-dimensional
Einstein--scalar two-brane boundary-value problem. The publishable result is
the pairing:

- validated existence and local uniqueness at an adjusted separation near
  `D = 1.9975`; and
- complete positive-curvature exclusion at the original fixed separation
  `D0 = 0.9996389747145851` over the full declared launch interval.

That pairing turns the previously observed separation mismatch into a proved
property of the reduced static model. It is not an event-horizon paper and is
not a proof of the full collapse or Lin scenario.

## Strongest defensible title

**Computer-assisted existence and fixed-separation exclusion in a
five-dimensional Einstein--scalar two-brane boundary-value problem**

## Strongest defensible central claim

For one source-pinned canonical Einstein--scalar two-wall model, rigorous
interval computations establish a unique reduced shooting solution when the
proper separation is allowed to adjust, while an exact interval cover excludes
every positive-curvature solution at the original fixed separation throughout
the declared launch domain. Therefore the nearly factor-of-two separation
mismatch is intrinsic to that fixed reduced static family rather than an
ordinary root-finding failure.

## What is already strong

- The two halves answer complementary questions under a common action,
  parameter set, orientation, and residual definition.
- D1 has a strict interval-Newton self-map, a derivative bounded away from
  zero, transverse event location, resolution nesting, and exact inclusion
  arithmetic.
- D2A covers the complete declared launch interval exactly, leaves no
  unresolved boxes, and has a uniform residual-vector floor much larger than
  roundoff or root-solver tolerance.
- The post-run and consolidation verifiers reproduce the decisive counts,
  inclusions, hashes, and checkpoint bindings.
- The claim boundary is unusually clean: the result is meaningful without
  needing the collapse or event-horizon program to succeed.

## Required additions before submission

### Proof authority

1. Add a producer-independent replay of the D1 interval ODE enclosure. The
   existing independent audit checks formulas and exact inclusion arithmetic,
   but it consumes the producer's enclosures.
2. Seal complete interval enclosures for reconstructed geometric quantities:
   positive curvature `k`, conformal and proper lengths, warp positivity and
   minimum, the first integral over the full domain, and both wall identities.
3. Present a formal reduction lemma from the action and junction conventions
   to the reduced IVP, first-event map, and fixed-distance residuals.
4. State the interval-Newton and interval-cover propositions in ordinary
   theorem-proof form, with the software certificate as evidence rather than
   as the prose structure of the paper.

### Reproducibility

5. Produce a clean public source package containing the minimal verifier,
   exact input literals, software versions, commands, certificate records, and
   a stable archival identifier.
6. Demonstrate the independent replay in a clean environment and include one
   compact human-auditable table of all proof-bearing intervals.

### Literature and exposition

7. Complete forward/backward citation chaining around the closest
   scalar-brane and computer-assisted Einstein papers.
8. Explain why this parameter set is mathematically or physically worth
   certifying, without claiming that the dynamical collapse selects it.
9. Keep the main article vertical: model and theorem, proof architecture,
   decisive certificates, comparison with prior work, and limitations. Put
   full manifests and operational records in the archive or supplement.

Linear stability would materially strengthen the physical interpretation but
is not required for a narrowly framed existence/exclusion theorem, provided
the paper states that stability and dynamical selection are open.

## Fatal weaknesses if not corrected

- Calling the current result a theorem for the full five-dimensional geometry
  while the D1 ODE enclosure lacks an independent implementation replay and
  the reconstructed geometric identities are not fully enclosed.
- Presenting the adjusted static solution as the endpoint of the authenticated
  collapse evolution without a same-development bridge.
- Advertising an event horizon, baby universe, radion transition, or full Lin
  mechanism from the static BVP certificates.
- Claiming universal nonexistence when D2A is limited to positive curvature,
  the fixed action, the exact `D0`, and `0 <= a <= v0`.
- Claiming that computer-assisted Einstein existence is itself novel.

Any one of the first four framing errors would convert a viable specialist
paper into a likely rejection on scope or proof-authority grounds.

## Audience and journal class

The natural audience is mathematical relativity, rigorous or validated
numerics, and warped-brane boundary-value problems. The current contribution
fits a specialist mathematical-relativity or computational-gravity venue
better than a broad high-energy-physics journal. Plausible journal classes
include *General Relativity and Gravitation*, *Journal of Mathematical
Physics*, *Classical and Quantum Gravity*, or a rigorous-numerics venue with a
geometry audience. Exact targeting should wait until the independent D1 replay
and literature closure are complete.

## Editorial recommendation

Proceed, but begin a new, narrow manuscript rather than inserting this result
into the horizon manuscript. Do not lead with the research history or with the
number of checks. Lead with the mathematical mismatch: the same fixed wall
model has a rigorously certified positive-curvature solution only after the
proper separation is allowed to move, while the original separation is
rigorously excluded over the complete declared launch domain.
