# Source-pinned novelty assessment

Search date: 2026-10-02.

## Search question

The targeted question was whether the literature already contains a rigorous,
computer-assisted result for a warped five-dimensional Einstein--scalar
two-wall boundary-value problem that combines:

1. validated shooting existence at an adjusted separation; and
2. complete fixed-separation nonexistence or exclusion over a declared launch
   interval.

The search deliberately separated three issues: the established physics of
bulk-scalar stabilization, computer-assisted existence in Einstein systems,
and the specific existence/exclusion pairing proved here.

## Closest model literature

- W. D. Goldberger and M. B. Wise, [*Modulus Stabilization with Bulk Fields*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9907447v2),
  arXiv:hep-ph/9907447v2, Phys. Rev. Lett. 83, 4922--4925 (1999),
  DOI `10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.4922`. This establishes the bulk-scalar
  stabilization mechanism and brane-localized potentials; it does not provide
  the present validated existence/exclusion certificate.
- O. DeWolfe, D. Z. Freedman, S. S. Gubser, and A. Karch, [*Modeling the fifth
  dimension with scalars and gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9909134v4), arXiv:hep-th/9909134v4, Phys. Rev. D 62,
  046008 (2000), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevD.62.046008`. This is the closest
  conceptual precedent for nonlinear scalar--gravity backgrounds with flat or
  curved maximally symmetric branes and dynamically selected spacing. It does
  not report a rigorous interval-Newton proof plus a complete fixed-spacing
  interval exclusion for the present wall model.
- P. Kanti, K. A. Olive, and M. Pospelov, [*Static Solutions for Brane Models
  with a Bulk Scalar Field*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0002229v3), arXiv:hep-ph/0002229v3, Phys. Lett. B 481,
  386--396 (2000), DOI `10.1016/S0370-2693(00)00465-2`. This supplies exact
  and analytic static-solution context and emphasizes parameter correlations;
  its model and result are not the present validated two-wall theorem.
- J. K. Ghosh, E. Kiritsis, F. Nitti, and L. T. Witkowski, [*De Sitter and
  Anti-de Sitter branes in self-tuning models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.09794v2), arXiv:1807.09794v2, JHEP 11
  (2018) 128, DOI `10.1007/JHEP11(2018)128`. This studies curved branes and
  existence/nonexistence regimes in a different holographic self-tuning
  framework, not the fixed canonical two-wall problem certified here.
- K. Farakos and P. Pasipoularides, [*Second Randall--Sundrum brane world
  scenario with a nonminimally coupled bulk scalar field*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0602200v2),
  arXiv:hep-th/0602200v2, Phys. Rev. D 73, 084012 (2006),
  DOI `10.1103/PhysRevD.73.084012`; and S. Pal and S. Kar, [*de Sitter branes
  with a bulk scalar*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0701266v3), arXiv:hep-th/0701266v3, Gen. Relativ. Gravit. 41,
  1165--1179 (2009), DOI `10.1007/s10714-008-0697-2`. These are useful nearby
  numerical or exact curved-brane constructions, but they change the scalar
  coupling or matter model and do not duplicate the validated pairing.

## Closest rigorous-computation literature

- M. Reiterer and E. Trubowitz, [*Choptuik's Critical Spacetime Exists*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.3766v1),
  arXiv:1203.3766v1, Commun. Math. Phys. 368, 143--186 (2019),
  DOI `10.1007/s00220-019-03413-8`. It is an important computer-assisted
  Einstein--massless-scalar existence proof. It shows that rigorous numerical
  existence in general relativity is not itself novel; its spacetime,
  equations, and proof architecture differ substantially from the present
  brane BVP.
- T. Buttsworth and L. Hodgkinson, [*Computationally-assisted proof of a novel
  O(3) x O(10)-invariant Einstein metric on S12*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01184v1), arXiv:2412.01184v1, J. Lond.
  Math. Soc. 113 (2026) e70477, DOI `10.1112/jlms.70477`, and Q. S. Wang,
  [*Computer-assisted construction of SU(2)-invariant negative Einstein
  metrics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21644v2), arXiv:2504.21644v2, Ann. Global Anal. Geom. 69 (2026) 18,
  DOI `10.1007/s10455-026-10037-4`. Both demonstrate current use of rigorous
  numerics to establish Einstein metrics. Neither concerns scalar-brane
  junction conditions or pairs a variable-separation root with a complete
  fixed-separation exclusion.

## Search coverage and negative finding

Targeted searches combined terms including `interval Newton`, `validated
numerics`, `computer-assisted proof`, `Einstein scalar`, `warped brane`,
`two brane`, `fixed separation`, `shooting`, and `nonexistence`. Searches were
then traced to primary arXiv records or publisher metadata. No close precedent
was located for the full combination of model, proof method, and paired
existence/exclusion statement.

This is a bounded literature finding, not proof of global priority. A formal
manuscript should still run citation chaining forward and backward from the
closest model papers and search MathSciNet/INSPIRE/zbMATH before claiming
priority.

## Novelty judgment

### Mathematical novelty

**Moderate and conditional.** Computer-assisted Einstein existence is already
established. The potentially new mathematical contribution is the specific
certificate architecture: a transverse first-event reduction, strict
interval-Newton existence and local uniqueness, paired with an exact complete
cover excluding the corresponding positive-curvature solution at a second
fixed separation.

### Model-specific novelty

**Strongest part of the case.** No located source gives this result for the
fixed canonical five-dimensional Einstein--scalar two-wall model and the
declared parameter set. The factor-of-two separation mismatch is converted
from a numerical observation into a rigorous statement within the reduced
static family.

### Interpretive novelty

**Limited.** Curved branes, bulk-scalar stabilization, and separation selection
are established ideas. The certificate does not explain a dynamical transition
between the two separations and does not connect the static root to the
collapse lineage. Event-horizon, baby-universe, and broader cosmological
interpretations are not supported by this theorem.
