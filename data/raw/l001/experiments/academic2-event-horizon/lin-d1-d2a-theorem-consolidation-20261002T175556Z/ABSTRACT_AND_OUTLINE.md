# Prospective abstract and article outline

## Abstract

We give a computer-assisted existence and exclusion result for a fixed
five-dimensional Einstein--scalar model on a two-brane interval. After
reducing the warped maximally symmetric field equations and junction
conditions to a one-parameter shooting problem, validated interval integration
and a strict interval-Newton inclusion establish a unique local shooting root
whose proper wall separation lies in
`[1.99749755859375, 1.99761962890625]`. For the same action, wall parameters,
orientation, and launch family, an exact adaptive interval cover of the full
declared domain `0 <= a <= 0.1` excludes every positive-curvature solution at
the original separation `D0 = 0.9996389747145851`; no unresolved boxes remain,
and the upper-wall residual vector has a uniform infinity-norm lower bound
greater than `0.0077` wherever positive curvature is admissible. Thus the
nearly factor-of-two separation mismatch is intrinsic to this reduced static
family rather than a missed shooting root or ordinary numerical tolerance.
The theorem does not establish stability, dynamical selection, a radion
transition, or any collapse or event-horizon statement.

## Article outline

### 1. Introduction

- State the concrete problem: whether one fixed scalar-wall action admits the
  required positive-curvature static background at the original separation.
- State the paired answer immediately: existence after separation adjustment,
  exclusion at the original separation.
- Distinguish the result from established stabilization mechanisms and from
  the separate collapse program.

### 2. Fixed Einstein--scalar two-brane model

- Give the doubled-cover action and exact parameter literals.
- Define the maximally symmetric warped ansatz and proper-distance variables.
- Derive junction orientations and the first integral.
- State the provenance asymmetry of the lower and upper tensions.

### 3. Reduction to two rigorous problems

- Define the lower-wall launch map and curvature.
- Define `G`, the first transverse event `D(a)`, `h`, and `q(a)`.
- State the variable-separation root theorem.
- Define the fixed-distance residual vector and admissible launch domain.
- State the fixed-separation exclusion theorem.

### 4. Validated existence certificate

- Describe interval flow and event localization.
- Derive sensitivity and implicit-event formulas.
- Give the frozen interval, strict interval-Newton self-map, derivative bounds,
  event bracket, and resolution nesting.
- Explain exactly what local uniqueness means.

### 5. Validated exclusion certificate

- Describe the exact adaptive cover and discharge rules.
- Give coverage proof, box counts, zero unresolved boxes, and uniform residual
  floor.
- Explain why the result is complete only on the declared positive-curvature
  launch domain.

### 6. Independent verification and reproducibility

- Report the independent formula/inclusion audit and D2A post-run audit.
- Add the required producer-independent D1 replay.
- Present the minimal certificate table; move complete records and hashes to
  the archive.

### 7. Relation to earlier work

- Compare with Goldberger--Wise stabilization and nonlinear scalar-gravity
  warped backgrounds.
- Compare proof methodology with computer-assisted Einstein constructions.
- Identify the model-specific novelty of the paired existence/exclusion
  result without claiming general novelty for rigorous computation in GR.

### 8. Interpretation and limitations

- Interpret the mismatch as a property of the reduced static family.
- State that stability, radion dynamics, same-development selection, and all
  horizon or baby-universe implications remain open.
- Identify the next mathematical step as independent enclosure replay and
  completion of derived geometric certificates.
