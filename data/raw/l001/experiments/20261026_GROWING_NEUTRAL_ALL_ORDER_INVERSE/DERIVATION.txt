Conditional real-axis inverse for all neutral harmonics n>=2
===========================================================
This is not a nonlinear-family convergence proof. The background tubes,
selected eigenmode, and compatible scalar reduction are inherited. The
calculation never imports background enclosures from the failed predecessor.

The scalar equation is f''+A f'-V f=F, with outward Robin data
  -f'(a)+beta_a f(a)=g_a, f'(b)+beta_b f(b)=g_b.
Here A=4H+BT/D, V=m-mu_n E^2+2T^2+B T'/D,
  mu_n=-nr(nr+3h), B=12HT-4mPhi,
  J=3(-mu_n-4h^2)E^2, D=J+2T^2.
For r>h>0 and n>=2, J>=3 n^2 r^2 E^2>0.
The conservative original Robin lower bound is
  beta>=(21-1/(2-mu_n E^2))*D/(2J)-|2TT'|/J
       >=41/4-|2TT'|/J.

Use the fixed positive rescaling f=E^2 y=exp(-2W)y. Direct differentiation
gives Atilde=BT/D and
  Vtilde=m-mu_n E^2+(4/3)T^2-2h^2E^2+4H^2
         +B(T'+2HT)/D.
The lower and upper Robin coefficients become beta_a+2H(a) and
beta_b-2H(b), respectively. Requiring beta-2|H|>0 on all cells is a
conservative sufficient condition for both walls.

The archived cells yield exact outward constants alpha,b>0, K,KA,Kbeta>=0,
and real lower bounds L0 and beta0 (not assumed positive), such that
  Vtilde >= alpha*n^2+b*n+L0-K/n^2,
  |Atilde| <= KA/n^2,
  betatilde >= beta0-Kbeta/n^2.
K,Kbeta,KA are absolute numerator bounds divided by 3*rlo^2*Elo^2.
The program also obtains |Vtilde|<=u2*n^2+u1*n+U0+K/n^2.
All extrema use fresh background cells and the full selected-rate interval.

For c=alpha/2, checking
  alpha*N^2/2+b*N+L0-K/N^2>=0
proves Vtilde>=c*n^2 for every n>=N, because the left expression is
monotone increasing. Set A=KA/N^2 and
v=u2+u1/N+U0/N^2+K/N^4. These imply |Atilde|<=A and |Vtilde|<=v*n^2.
Search only N=9,...,128 with the fixed rational lambda=1/128. Require
  betatilde>=1, c-lambda^2-A*lambda/N>=0,
  lambda*N*Lmin>=1, N*Lmin>=2, 1-A/(2N)>0.
No cutoff found is method inconclusiveness, not a spectral conclusion.

Let F0=||F_y||infty, G=max|g_y| and S=F0+nG. The positive barrier
  w=F0/(c*n^2)+(2G/(lambda*n))
     [exp(-lambda*n*(s-a))+exp(-lambda*n*(b-s))]
satisfies (-d^2-Atilde*d+Vtilde)w>=F0. At each wall its outward
derivative is 2G(1-exp(-lambda*n*L))>=G; nonnegative Robin terms help.
The maximum principle applied to w+-y therefore gives
  n^2||y||<=C0*S, C0=max(1/c,4/lambda).
One-sided Taylor over length 1/n (available everywhere when nL>=2),
combined with the ODE and absorption, gives
  n||y'||<=C1*S,
  C1=[(2+v/2)C0+1/2]/[1-A/(2N)].
Then ||y''||<=[A*C1/N+v*C0+1]S=C2*S.
This establishes existence/uniqueness by regular second-order BVP theory:
maximum-principle uniqueness implies invertibility of the two-dimensional
boundary map. It does not require a sign assumption on the forcing.

For finite 2<=n<N use saved direct n=2..8 bounds or the displayed analytic
envelopes at 9<=n<N. With Vmin>0,beta_min>0 define
  l=min(Lmin/2,1/(A+1)),
  c0=max(1/Vmin,1/(n*beta_min)),
  c1=[(2/l+l*Vabs/2)c0+l/2]/[1-A*l/2],
  c2=A*c1+Vabs*c0+1.
Then n^2||y||+n||y'||+||y''|| <= (n^2*c0+n*c1+c2)S.
Every finite order must pass; unresolved bounds remain unresolved.

Transfer back to f: F_y=F/E^2 and g_y=g/E^2 at the walls. Let
Emin2<=E^2<=Emax2, |H|<=H0, |H'|<=Hp0. Since
  f'=E^2(y'-2Hy),
  f''=E^2[y''-4Hy'+(4H^2-2H')y],
and n>=2, a safe unoptimized transfer for the summed weighted norm is
  C_f=(Emax2/Emin2)*max(1+H0+H0^2+Hp0/2,1+2H0,1)*C_y.
Here C_y is the maximum of the high-order and every finite-order constant.
The scalar bound presupposes compatible reduced forcing; it is not an
inverse for arbitrary mutually inconsistent Einstein residuals. It supplies
neither the metric/source norm bridge nor a complex-collar estimate.
