# The remaining physical-tachyon band

Status: prospective analytic diagnostic and ranked next gate. No numerical execution is authorized or performed by this note. The new exact index closes μ<-4k, while possible additional physical tachyons lie in `-4k<=μ<0`.

The endpoint phase therefore extends continuously from the accepted negative-shifted phase through the whole remaining band. Its off-shell Green derivative remains

    dθ_b/dμ=Z/[2a_b⁴(f_b²+L_b²)]>0.

Let `θ_A=θ_b(-4k)` be the lifted auxiliary phase already fixed by the turning-count theorem, and let `θ_E=θ_b(0)` be its continuous extension to zero physical mass. With `α_b=atan(2c_b)`, every additional tachyon in `(-4k,0)` is exactly one crossing of

    θ_b=jπ-α_b,      j>=n+1,

strictly before θ_E. Thus the additional count equals the number of these angles strictly less than θ_E. If θ_E is itself an upper-wall angle, that root is massless and is not counted as a tachyon. In particular, the sufficient-and-necessary no-additional-tachyon criterion is

    θ_E<=(n+1)π-α_b,

where equality permits a massless mode at μ=0. Strict inequality excludes that massless endpoint as well.

This reduction does not evaluate θ_E. A single sign of G(0) supplies only parity of the crossings after the auxiliary endpoint and cannot exclude an unseen pair.
