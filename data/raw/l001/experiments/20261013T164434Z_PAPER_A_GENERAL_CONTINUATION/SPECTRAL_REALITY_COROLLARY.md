# Reality of regular admissible eigenvalues

Status: proposed short corollary for independent review. This uses the regular Green identity and real boundary conditions; it does not construct a self-adjoint operator domain or prove completeness.

Allow a nonzero regular admissible profile `(ψ,f,g)` to be complex, with a potentially complex μ, on the real background and for real Robin or Dirichlet wall parameters of the main theorem. Its complex conjugate is a profile at μ̄ on the same background. The polynomial Green identity is valid for complex profiles as well as real ones. Pairing the profile with its conjugate and integrating gives

    J_b-J_a=(μ-μ̄)∫₀ᵇ a²(|f|²+6|ψ|²)ds.

At a finite Robin wall, both profiles obey the same real relation `L=±κ f/2`, so their antisymmetric flux vanishes. At a Dirichlet wall both f values vanish, and the same flux vanishes. Thus J_a=J_b=0 for any combination of these walls. The remaining integral is strictly positive for a nonzero profile: if f and ψ vanished identically, then g=f' would vanish identically as well. Therefore μ=μ̄.

Every regular admissible mode eigenvalue of this boundary problem is consequently real. The exact turning-count theorem, if accepted, counts every admissible eigenvalue in μ<-4k without an additional assumption that a candidate negative-shifted eigenvalue is real. It still gives no count in μ>=-4k, no spectral completeness, and no nonlinear conclusion. Reality follows here from the regular positive bilinear identity and declared boundary conditions, without importing a singular reduced Sturm–Liouville domain.
