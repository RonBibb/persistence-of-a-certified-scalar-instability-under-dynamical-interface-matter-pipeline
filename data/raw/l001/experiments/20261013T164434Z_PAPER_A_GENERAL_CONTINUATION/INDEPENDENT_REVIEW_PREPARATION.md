## Independent hand checks of the prospective seed mechanism

Write T=Phi_s, H=a_s/a, T_s=V'(Phi)-4HT, H_s=-T²/3-k/a², and lambda=-q². Differentiating the background gives

T_ss=-4H T_s+(V''+4k/a²+4T²/3)T.

The constrained regular scalar system has D=2T²+3q²/a²>0 and constraint C_reg=Tg-T_s f+D psi. Eliminating psi gives

f_s=g,
g_s=P_q g+Q_q f,
P_q=-4H-C T/D,
Q_q=q²/a²+V''+4k/a²+2T²+C T_s/D,
C=12HT-4V'=-4(T_s+HT).

These signs agree with independent substitution. Differentiating C_reg while imposing the scalar equation gives the off-shell relation

(C_reg)_s=-4H C_reg+D(psi_s+2H psi+Tf/3).

Therefore solving the eliminated scalar equation and defining psi algebraically recovers the regular momentum equation; equivalence does not divide by T.

For fixed r>0, the cone h=g-rf>=0 has inward derivative h_s=(Q_q+P_q r-r²)f at h=0. Strict positivity of this coefficient, positive initial g, nonnegative initial f, and initial h>=0 imply positive f and g downstream on a nonzero interval. At the upper endpoint,

g+2T psi=(3q²g/a²+2T T_s f)/D.

Thus the proposed large-q lower bound on g/f makes the upper residual positive, including the upper Dirichlet face. A frozen proof still needs uniform bounds, the admissible launch inequalities on all lower reciprocal stiffnesses, and the opposite auxiliary sign.
