Compatible all-neutral-order real-axis reconstruction
====================================================
For n>=2 and mu=-nr(nr+3h), put
  D=2T^2-3(mu+4k)E^2, J=D-2T^2, B=12HT-4mPhi.
The homogeneous radial operator is supplemented by compatible sources:
  p'=-2Hp-Tf/3+Sp,
  f'=g,
  g'=-4Hg+(m-mu E^2+2T^2)f+Bp+Sg,
  Tg-T'f+Dp=Sc.

Reconstruct p=(Sc-Tf'+T'f)/D. Eliminating it gives
  f''+(4H+BT/D)f'-(m-mu E^2+2T^2+BT'/D)f
    = Sg+BSc/D.
Differentiating the reconstructed p and using the reduced scalar equation
gives, exactly,
  p'-(-2Hp-Tf/3+Sp)=(Sc'+4HSc-TSg-DSp)/D.
The identity is qualified by clearing D^2 in an exact sparse polynomial
ring with a general mu; no special negative forced rate is substituted.
Compatibility is a hypothesis on the source data, not an optional check.

With sigma=+1 at the lower wall and -1 at the upper wall, the original
Robin equation is 2sigma(f'+2Tp)-Gamma f=R, Gamma=21-1/(2-mu E^2).
The outward scalar equation is
  -sigma f'+beta_sigma f=g_sigma,
  beta_sigma=Gamma D/(2J)-2sigma TT'/J,
  g_sigma=-D R/(2J)+2sigma TSc/J.
The exact qualifier multiplies the reduced row by 2J and compares it with
minus D times the original row, checking both wall orientations.

All norms below are real-axis supremum norms. Fresh authenticated cells
give bounds H0,Hp0,T0,Tp0,B0 and d0=3*r_min^2*min(E^2)>0, with
D,J>=d0*n^2. Write Rmax=max over both walls of |R| and
  L_n=||Sc||+||Sg||+n Rmax.
Then the scalar forcing/load norm S_n=||F||+n max|g_sigma| obeys
  S_n <= Csource L_n,
  Csource=max(1, B0/(4d0)+T0/d0, 1/2+T0^2/(4d0)).
Indeed D/J<=1+2T0^2/(d0*n^2), and n>=2. The previously certified neutral
scalar inverse gives U_n(f)<=Cf*Csource L_n, where
  U_n(v)=n^2||v||+n||v'||+||v''||.

For metric reconstruction define
  R_m=||Sc||+U_n(f)+n||Sp||+||Sp'||.
No derivative is gained for free: Sc must be differentiable for the
compatibility identity, and Sp' is an explicit norm input for p''.
The constraint gives n^2||p||<=P0 R_m with
  P0=max(1,T0/2+Tp0/4)/d0.
The compatible momentum equation gives n||p'||<=P1 R_m with
  P1=H0 P0+T0/6+1.
Differentiating that equation gives ||p''||<=P2 R_m with
  P2=Hp0 P0/2+H0 P1+Tp0/12+T0/6+1.
Thus U_n(p)<=(P0+P1+P2)R_m.

For a single input norm
  Q_n=||Sc||+||Sg||+nRmax+n||Sp||+||Sp'||,
let Cscalar=Cf*Csource. Then
  U_n(f)<=Cscalar Q_n,
  U_n(p)<=[P0+P1+P2]*(1+Cscalar)Q_n.
These are intentionally unoptimized bounds. They inherit the accepted
background and scalar inverse certificate. They do not bound c, b=c-2p,
complex norms, nonlinear source compositions, or the actual analytic
family radius. In particular source compatibility for an all-order
nonlinear construction must still be proved inductively from the field
equations; this packet qualifies the linear response identity it needs.
