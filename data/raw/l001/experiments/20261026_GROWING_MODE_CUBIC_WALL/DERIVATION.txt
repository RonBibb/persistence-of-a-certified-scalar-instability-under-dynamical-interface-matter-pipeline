Convention: eta=epsilon exp(r t), ordinary coefficients, r>h>0;
both conserved currents vanish. First-order tracefree and Israel equations
give b1=-2p1 and xi1=0. Fields p_j,f_j,b_j are Eulerian; chi_j is intrinsic
to the wall. Set x=xi2, p=p1, P=p2, c=c2, f=f1, F=f2, g=f1', G=f2',
u=p1', gp=f1'', chi=chi1 and chi2=the second wall oscillator coefficient.
Use sigma=+1 at the lower wall and sigma=-1 at the upper wall.

The wall shift is eta^2 x+eta^3 xi3. Consequently
  log(a(S)/a_i) = eta^2 Hx+eta^3 Hxi3,
  p(S) = eta p+eta^2 P+eta^3(p3+xu),
  b(S) = -2eta p+eta^2(c-2P)+eta^3(c3-2p3-2xu),
  deltaPhi(S) = eta f+eta^2(F+Tx)+eta^3(f3+Txi3+xg).
Velocity begins at eta^2, so all boost corrections begin at eta^4 and
cannot contribute to the displayed cubic rows. This is an order argument,
not a replacement of the exact equations by a low-velocity model.

Tracefree Israel, including oscillator kinetic stress, has cubic coefficient
  3 sigma E^2 r(3r-h) xi3 + qI3,
  qI3 = 4 sigma E^2 r(2h-7r) x p
        -2r^2 E^2 chi chi2 + r^2 E^2 p chi^2.
Thus xi3=-qI3/[3 sigma E^2 r(3r-h)]. The first term is geometric,
the remaining terms are the oscillator stress. No current term remains.

The oscillator and normalized scalar-normal source coefficients are
  o3 = E^2[-4r(r+3h)p chi2
           +2r(r-3h)(P+Hx-p^2)chi] - xg,
  j3 = x gp + 2p(G+T' x) + (2P-c+2p^2)g
       +T[2xu+4pP-2pc+(4/3)p^3]
       +2r^2 E^2 x f -(21sigma/2)xg.
The tilt term 2r^2 E^2 x f and displaced scalar potential term
-(21sigma/2)xg are required. In particular xi1=0 does not remove cubic
transport: xi2 already moves the first-order scalar and metric fields.

Set mu3=-3r(3r+3h), d3=2-mu3 E^2 and Gamma3=21-1/d3. The full new rows are
  d3 chi3-f3-Txi3+o3=0,
  g3+T'xi3-Tc3+2Tp3-sigma[21(f3+Txi3)-chi3]/2+j3=0.
Their eliminated Robin load is
  R3=2sigma[Tc3-(T'-sigma Gamma3 T/2)xi3-j3+sigma o3/(2d3)],
  2sigma(g3+2Tp3)-Gamma3 f3=R3.
The qualifier checks elimination without symbolic division, multiplying
the Robin equation by d3. Positive r>h gives nonzero graph divisor and
positive oscillator divisor; the radial cubic inverse is a separate gate.

The Eulerian bulk tracefree row independently gives
  qT3=4r^2(6pP+fF-3pc),
  c3=-4r(6pP+fF-3pc)/[3(3r-h)].
The new xi3 and c3 must be supplied to the Robin load; do not omit them
merely because the source coefficients exclude new unknowns.
