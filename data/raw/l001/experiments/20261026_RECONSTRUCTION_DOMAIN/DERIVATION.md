# Analytic reconstruction domain

The audited coefficient envelope supplies a positive domain for summing the compatible bulk fields, interface graphs and surface oscillators. The following sufficient inequalities keep their coordinate map and physical wall factors regular. The conclusion is conditional on the inherited analytic recursion and source estimates; it is not a distance-tail or scalar-turn certificate.

## Radius and spatial reserve

Use the coefficient norm on the working stadium of radius rho = 1/40000. The background is holomorphic on the larger stadium R = 2 rho. Write r for the audited majorant radius in w = z C_N and s for its uniform bound. Choose r_phys = r/32. Then 16 |w| ≤ r/2 and the nonnegative envelope obeys Y(16 |z|) ≤ M(16 |w|) ≤ s.

Start on the stadium of radius rho/2 around the real interval of length D. The affine graph X has |X| ≤ Cx s, with Cx = 1 + rho/(2 Dmin), and |X_r| ≤ s/Dmin. Checking Cx s < rho/4 leaves shifted points in the stadium of radius 3 rho/4, where the original working weight d is at least 1/4. Thus the bulk value, first radial derivative and second radial derivative series are bounded by s, 4s and 16s, respectively. The dilation 16 absorbs d^(-2n); this argument uses the new working norm, not the earlier outer-collar norm.

For D0 = -6h z partial_z and g = 3/25 ≥ 6h, the bounds for D0 u and D0² u at fixed pulled coordinate are g s and g² s on that same restricted domain. For total derivative order at most two the mixed bound |partial_r D0 u| ≤ 4g s also follows. Graph and oscillator endpoint values, first time derivatives and second time derivatives have the bounds s, g s and g² s. A strict factor-two reserve in w permits arbitrary fixed further derivatives on compact subsets. These estimates do not identify D0 with the physical fixed-position derivative D = D0 − W partial_r.

## Regular geometry and wall factors

Set Ebar = 8 + 1/1000 and use the inherited background Tbar. At the physical real walls, |p| and |b| are at most s. The exact rational inequality 1/(1−2s) < 2 bounds exp(2s). Consequently

    |v| ≤ 2 Ebar g s < 1/4
    |1−v²| ≥ 1−(2 Ebar g s)²
    |J_r| ≥ 1−s/Dmin
    |S_b−S_a| ≥ Dmin−s.

For real fields these give timelike, separated walls and an orientation-preserving affine radial map. The analytic lapse and warp factors remain nonzero because the background factors are nonzero on their authenticated collar and exponentials have no zeros. Their real positive signs are preserved.

For the selected coupling c = 1−(Phi−Phi*)/1000, the physical endpoint shift and scalar perturbation give |c−1| ≤ (Tbar+1)s/1000 < 1/2. Here Phi* is the corresponding neutral wall value, and the background scalar derivative is bounded along the short displacement segment. A conservative background composition ratio is q = 2 Cx s/rho < 1/2.

## Meaning and remaining step

The norm bounds ensure normal convergence of the fields and the derivatives needed by the equations. The graph Jacobian, wall boost and selected coupling stay away from their singular values. Conditional on the inherited compatible all-order recursion, analytic substitution therefore has the prescribed zero equation coefficients on this domain. This does not establish arbitrary-data evolution or a global branch.

For fixed nonzero C_N the domain is |z| ≤ r_phys/C_N and the envelope comparison is Y(16|z|) ≤ M(16 C_N |z|); C_N = 0 is the neutral case. This is a sufficient local analytic domain, not a measured breakdown threshold. A quantitative distance remainder still requires bounding the distance functional on this domain and comparing its tail with the authenticated low-order coefficients. Preservation of the scalar turn requires its own sign and derivative margins.
