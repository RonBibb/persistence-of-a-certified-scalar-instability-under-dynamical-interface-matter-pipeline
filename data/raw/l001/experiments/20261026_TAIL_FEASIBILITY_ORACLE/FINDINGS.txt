Derived sharpening candidate
----------------------------
The exact neutral tracefree Israel equation can be normalized before
taking absolute values. Its lapse/warp prefactors cancel, leaving them
only through the wall velocity. The normalized graph inverse has divisor
nr(nr-h), with the inherited lower bound n^2/8192. This normalization was
independently checked algebraically for both wall orientations.

In addition, the selected branch has xi1=0. Its quadratic wall forcing
is the oscillator kinetic term, with exact relation
  xi_i,2 = sigma_i*r*chi_i,1^2/[4*(2r-h)].
It is therefore wasteful to assign the graph the same first-order scalar
envelope as every bulk variable. The two oscillators also need distinct
wall-specific bounds; the upper-wall first coefficient is not as small
as the lower-wall one.

terms, derivation and qualification requirements. This is a derived
route to a sharper estimate, not a replacement numerical certificate.
