Normalized neutral graph equation for the next estimate

Status: analytical successor candidate, not substituted into the sealed
majorant or a new physical-radius certificate. The measured BI share is
a share of the sufficient proof budget, not a physical force allocation.

Use the exact neutral S7 row with oscillator kinetic stress,

  R_I = sigma/(N sqrt(1-v^2)) [v_t/(1-v^2)+v(Db-h-Dp)]
        - chi_t^2/[2 N^2(1-v^2)] = 0,

where N=a(S)exp(p), B=exp(b), v=(B/N)S_t, sigma=+1 or -1.
All bulk fields and fixed-position derivatives in this expression are
evaluated at the same moving wall. D is the fixed-position time operator;
L is the physical radial derivative. Total wall derivatives obey

  b_t^wall = Db+(Lb)S_t,
  p_t^wall = Dp+(Lp)S_t,
  (log N)_t^wall = H S_t+p_t^wall.

On the already required nonzero-lapse, nondegenerate-metric, timelike
domain, multiply R_I by sigma N^2(1-v^2)^(3/2)/B. This factor is
nonzero. The resulting exactly equivalent equation is

  S_tt-h S_t = -(2-v^2)(Db-Dp)S_t
              +(H-Lb+Lp)S_t^2-h v^2 S_t
              +(sigma/2)exp(-b)sqrt(1-v^2) chi_t^2.

The square root is the branch equal to +1 at the static background.
For complex-amplitude estimates its analytic domain must be bounded;
real timelikeness alone is not a complex-domain certificate.

Derivation check: put w=S_t, q=S_tt, t=sqrt(1-v^2), A=Db-h-Dp.
Then (N/B)v_t=q+(Db-Dp+(Lb-Lp-H)w)w. After multiplication the row is

  q+(Db-Dp+(Lb-Lp-H)w)w+t^2 w A
       -sigma t chi_t^2/(2B).

Substituting t^2=1-v^2 yields the displayed normalized equation.
No dynamics, sign, wall orientation, boost or transport term is dropped.
The warp/lapse prefactors cancel before absolute-value bounds are taken;
their remaining effect through v is still retained.

At order n, the new graph divisor is nr(nr-h), rather than the raw
sigma E_i^2 nr(nr-h). For n>=2, r>h and r>=1/64 imply

  nr(nr-h) >= n^2 r^2/2 >= n^2/8192.

This is an inverse bound for the normalized row, not permission to reuse
the old raw source weight. Rebuild the graph source and its contribution
to all other response channels consistently.

For the selected branch xi_1=0. At quadratic order all geometric products
on the right vanish; the exact remaining equation is

  2r(2r-h) xi_i,2 = (sigma_i/2) r^2 chi_i,1^2,
  xi_i,2 = sigma_i r chi_i,1^2/[4(2r-h)].

Consequently |xi_i,2| <= |chi_i,1|^2/4. The E_i^2 factor cancels exactly.
Use separate bounds at each wall: the small lower-wall oscillator value
must not be applied to the upper wall. The first graph envelope starts
at order two; the oscillator envelopes start at order one. Higher-order
metric-graph, oscillator-graph and boost contributions remain mandatory.
