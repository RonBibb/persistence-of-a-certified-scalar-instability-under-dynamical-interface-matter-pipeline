# Requirements for one central causal bracket

The minimum claim is one intersection of one connected launch curve with one
end-relative horizon.  It is not a horizon cross-section or an event-horizon
reconstruction.

## Frozen identity

All records must bind the same:

- physical development and time orientation;
- action, branes, corners and admitted boundary-contact class;
- exterior ID and its causal-past equality authority;
- launch slice and connected curve;
- physical length definition; and
- continuum/numerical error budget.

## Captured endpoint

For \(p_c\), require a theorem giving

\[
 p_c\notin J^-(E).
\]

The finite-barrier route must bind exact or rigorously enclosed source
expansions, NCC, umbilic contacts, causal closedness, separation, and strict
all-future target expansion.  One ray later entering a trapped region is not a
captured-event authority.

## Visible endpoint

For \(p_v\), require a continuum causal curve to a certified
\(W\subset E\).  A discrete trace, finite nonfailure or arrival at an
artificial wall is only a candidate.

## Bracket theorem

For a continuous curve \(\sigma:[0,1]\to\Sigma\) with
\(\sigma(0)=p_c\), \(\sigma(1)=p_v\), the captured endpoint lies outside
\(V_E=J^-(E)\) while the visible endpoint lies in its interior.  Connectedness
therefore forces at least one

\[
 \sigma(s_*)\in\partial J^-(E).
\]

The reported bracket width is the physical arc length of the certified
opposite-fate interval on \(\Sigma\), including its enclosure error.  A
coordinate-width threshold is insufficient.

For the exact warped-Kottler control on \(\tau=v-r=\)constant and fixed
\(z=z_0\), this width is

\[
 \psi(z_0)\int_{r_-}^{r_+}\sqrt{2-f(r)}\,dr,
\]

not merely \(r_+-r_-\).

## Production progression

1. Qualify one central pair at a fixed compact coordinate away from both rims.
2. Repeat at a second evolution resolution and independently solved outer
   domain.
3. Only then extend to a noncrossing family in \(z\).
4. A full cross-section requires coverage of the complete compact interval and
   one-sided rim enclosures; finitely many meridional samples do not suffice.

The exact warped-Kottler control meets the one-pair logic.  The current
collapse lineage meets none of the claim-grade endpoint authorities after the
compatibility failure.
