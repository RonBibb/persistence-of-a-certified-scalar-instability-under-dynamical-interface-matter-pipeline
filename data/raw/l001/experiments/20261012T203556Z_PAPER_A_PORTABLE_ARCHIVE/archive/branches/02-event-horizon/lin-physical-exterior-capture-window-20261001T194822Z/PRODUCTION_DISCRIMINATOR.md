# Production discriminator and stopping rules

## What has been decided

The finite-barrier architecture is not empty or internally contradictory.  On
an exact warped-Kottler family it supplies, simultaneously:

- the complete all-future barrier sign with \(\mathcal E=0\);
- a physical outer region and exact causal-past equality;
- an exact future-trapped cap;
- an exact continuum visible curve; and
- one central end-relative horizon bracket.

Thus the blocker is no longer the logical compatibility of those ingredients.
It is whether an exterior in this exact class, or a controlled perturbation of
it, belongs to the same development as the collapse.

## Decision sequence

### D1 — rigorous curved-background enclosure

Use the retained fine collocation polynomial as the center of an interval
Newton or radii-polynomial proof for the six-condition curved warp/scalar BVP.
The output must enclose \(k,L,D,\psi,\Phi\), positivity of \(\psi\), the first
integral, and all wall conditions.

- **Pass:** the Kottler replacement becomes an exact model-family result.
- **Fail/inconclusive:** the present route remains a conditional numerical
  control; do not run an event-horizon reconstruction on it.

This test is independent of the collapse evolution and can proceed while its
initial-data repair is unresolved.

### D2 — separation/lineage bridge

The candidate's physical separation is approximately 1.99757, versus about
0.99964 for the old selector.  Supply either:

1. a same-action fixed-separation curved solution at the collapse boundary
   data; or
2. a coupled radion/exterior evolution showing that the physical end reaches
   the enclosed curved branch while preserving the brane and corner
   conditions.

Parameter counting alone is not a nonexistence proof.  A failed finite BVP
search is also not a no-go.

At fixed original separation and fixed wall/action parameters, the
proper-distance reduction has one shooting parameter but two upper-wall
residuals. The cheap D2A study is therefore naturally an exclusion or
near-miss discriminator: a complete validated cover can rule out a common
zero on its declared range, whereas a small two-residual norm cannot prove
existence. Freeing a second physical parameter would change the problem.

- **Pass:** a same-development exterior target exists.
- **Fail by proof:** retire this Kottler route for the collapse lineage while
  retaining it as a theorem control.
- **Numerical failure only:** classify as unresolved.

### D3 — perturbative worldtube authority

On a newly admitted compatible development, use a fixed physical cylinder and
retain the exact production combination

\[
 \mathcal E=
 \frac{A_r}{2A}+\frac{B_r}{B}-\frac{(V_0)_z}{A}
 +\frac{k^t}{k^r}\left(\frac{A_t}{2A}+\frac{B_t}{B}-\frac{(U_0)_z}{A}\right)
 +\frac{k^z}{k^r}\left(\frac{B_z}{B}-\frac{A_z}{2A}\right).
\]

Do not replace it with cone orientation or a truncated derivative list.  A
claim-grade numerical candidate needs two evolution resolutions, two
independently solved outer domains, continuous-time enclosures, and

\[
 \inf_{t,z}\mathcal E(t,R,z)>-2/R.
\]

An analytic all-future promotion additionally needs a continuation/stability
estimate around the exact exterior.  Finite-time persistence, however long,
is not that estimate.

### D4 — one central bracket before an atlas

Only after D1–D3 and a continuum trapped-cap enclosure:

1. certify one launch event as captured by the barrier theorem;
2. certify one event on the same connected launch curve as visible via a
   continuum curve into \(W\subset E\);
3. bind one exterior ID and its causal-past equality; and
4. report the physical arc-length bracket with its enclosure error.

Stop after this pair.  A full compact-coordinate atlas and terminal-null
locator are justified only if the central bracket succeeds.

## Stop conditions

- No exact curved-background enclosure: no Kottler physical authority.
- No same-development separation bridge: no transfer to the collapse.
- No all-future derivative estimate: finite-slab result only.
- No continuum captured or visible endpoint: no central bracket.
- Opposite endpoints with different exterior IDs: no horizon inference.
- Locator disagreement with a certified bracket: treat the locator as a
  different surface or an incomplete error budget; never average them.
