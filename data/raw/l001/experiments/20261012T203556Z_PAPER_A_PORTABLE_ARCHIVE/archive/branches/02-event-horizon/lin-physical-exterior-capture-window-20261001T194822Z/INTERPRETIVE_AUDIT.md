# Interpretive audit

## Objections tested

1. **Was the nonzero \(g_{vr}\) term dropped?** No. It enters the exact
   outward null vector; eight independent frame identities pass. The temporal
   derivative block it multiplies vanishes because the control is stationary.
2. **Was the compact warp treated as irrelevant merely because it is
   conformal?** No. Its potential contribution is retained through the
   \(k^z\) term. Section normality gives \(k^z=0\) in this exact family.
3. **Is \(\mathcal E=0\) a numerical cancellation?** No. Every constituent
   derivative in the exact expression vanishes separately for the Kottler
   product form. The result is symbolic.
4. **Does the old finite barrier sample establish this construction?** No.
   P407 is not an input. The only inherited numerical number used is the
   curved-background candidate's \(k\), and only to show a nonempty parameter
   instance.
5. **Does the numerical curved BVP establish an exact background?** No. It is
   a qualified numerical candidate, not a validated existence enclosure.
6. **Does changing the base keep the brane action fixed?** Conditionally yes:
   for an exact same-\(k\) Einstein base, the pure-potential Israel and scalar
   Robin data depend only on \(\psi,\Phi\) and remain unchanged. This would
   fail for undeclared localized kinetic or noncovariant brane terms.
7. **Is the mass \(M=1\) inferred from the collapse?** No. It is a control
   parameter. The Einstein-base replacement admits any sub-Nariai \(M\); no
   collapse mass matching is claimed.
8. **Is the static exterior the original warped-KK radial end?** No. Positive
   base curvature gives a black-hole/cosmological-horizon static region. The
   end-relative horizon question is changed and explicitly re-identified.
9. **Does the outer-end equality rely on a finite wall being infinity?** No.
   It is proved from exact outgoing causal curves entirely inside the physical
   static exterior. It does not identify the cylinder with infinity.
10. **Does one trapped point/ray certify launch capture?** The chosen cap is
    certified as a set: the exact control has timelike \(\nabla r\), forcing
    every future causal curve inside the black-hole region toward decreasing
    \(r\). This direct proof is the load-bearing capture authority for the
    exact bracket. The finite-barrier theorem is a separate production route
    and still requires its global closed-future hypothesis.
11. **Does one pair reconstruct a horizon surface?** No. It establishes one
    central intersection only. A compact-coordinate atlas remains separate.
12. **Can the construction be radially spliced onto the collapse exterior?**
    Not without solving the field and junction problem at the splice. No such
    shell or matching is introduced here.
13. **Is the control horizon the original free-boundary profile?** No.  Its
    sections are constant-\(r\) \(S^2\times[z_-,z_+]\) black-string caps.  A
    free-boundary, compact-dependent collapse profile would require the
    separate same-development bridge and bracket atlas.
14. **Does the Boolean authority enumeration prove the Kottler global causal
    hypotheses?** No. It verifies that a deliberately conservative declared
    gate set fails closed. The exact control's central bracket is instead
    supported by the direct captured/visible proofs above. A production use of
    the generic barrier route must still establish every theorem premise.

## Ruling

The warped-Kottler construction is a valid conditional exact-family
discriminator. It proves that the barrier/capture/visibility architecture can
close in the declared type of canonical scalar-wall theory once an exact
curved background and common development are supplied.

It is not current-collapse evidence. The load-bearing missing items are an
exact curved-background enclosure, a same-development separation/asymptotic
bridge, continuum cap authority, and a compatible evolution. Those omissions
are sufficient to keep the scientific classification at
`CONDITIONAL-MODEL-CONTROL-ONLY`.
