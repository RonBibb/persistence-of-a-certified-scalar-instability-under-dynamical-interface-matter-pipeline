# Scientific interpretation

## Positive result

The global causal program is not blocked because its required ingredients are
mutually incompatible.  They coexist exactly in a same-action conditional
family: curved scalar-wall background, Kottler Einstein base, all-future
positive cylinder, trapped cap, physical visible curve, causal-past equality,
and a central horizon bracket.

This matters because the previous work had isolated the all-future barrier as
an abstract missing hypothesis.  Here that hypothesis is discharged by a
specific mechanism: stationarity and same-\(k\) Einstein-base replacement
make the complete remainder \(\mathcal E\) vanish identically.

It also repairs a model-class mismatch in the earlier stationary black-string
control.  That control correctly tested null machinery conditional on a flat
warped background, but the declared wall parameters later excluded that
static flat-base antecedent.  The Kottler replacement uses the available
positive-curvature branch instead.  Its remaining weakness is exact existence
and lineage, not the Einstein-base algebra.

## Novelty boundary

The broad existence of warped black strings whose induced geometry is
Schwarzschild--(anti-)de Sitter is not new.  Examples have been constructed in
other five-dimensional scalar-tensor theories, including a positive-curvature
brane in [Kanti, Nakas and Pappas, arXiv:1807.06880](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06880)
and an anti-de Sitter brane in
[Nakas, Pappas and Kanti, arXiv:1904.00216](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.00216).
Those papers use non-minimal scalar-curvature couplings and do not establish
the present canonical two-brane background.  They do show that the geometry
should be presented as a control mechanism, not as a new black-string class.

The new result within this research program is narrower: the same-curvature
Einstein-base substitution closes the previously separated barrier,
captured-endpoint, visible-endpoint and causal-past-transfer requirements in
one exact conditional family.  Its scientific value is diagnostic.  It says
the causal architecture is coherent and moves the unresolved burden to exact
background existence and same-development transfer.

Black-string stability is not tested here.  An exact stationary solution can
still be dynamically irrelevant if an allowed Gregory--Laflamme-type or
coupled scalar-gravitational mode grows.  Stability therefore belongs after
the D1 existence and D2 lineage gates; it is not supplied by the exact
barrier sign.

## Change in the physical question

The declared wall parameters analytically obstruct the proposed regular
flat-base static vacuum, while the qualified numerical alternative has
positive four-dimensional curvature.  If that curvature branch is the
relevant exterior, the causal end is not the originally assumed
asymptotically flat warped-KK radial infinity.  It is a de Sitter-type static
region bounded by black-hole and cosmological horizons.  The event-horizon
definition, visible observers and terminal surfaces must be rebuilt relative
to that end.

This is a fork, not a reinterpretation of P407.  The available curved branch
also has a substantially different brane separation.

## What would make it physically consequential

The next load-bearing result is not another backward-null run.  It is one of:

1. a rigorous enclosure of the curved BVP plus a same-development bridge from
   admissible collapse data; or
2. a proof that no such bridge exists at the original separation and wall
   parameters.

The first outcome would provide a concrete physical target on which the
capture and central-bracket calculation can be executed.  The second would
retire this route for the collapse while preserving a clean theorem control.

## New separation mechanism

The factor-of-two separation difference is no longer merely a comparison of
two numerical outputs. The oriented scalar Robin conditions require a
successful positive branch with endpoint fields below their wall targets to
decrease and then increase exactly once. The proper-distance flux
\(J=e^{4A}S\) bounds how much the negative lower-wall slope can recover.
For the declared parameters, launches below
\(a=0.0989750001\) cannot turn at any finite proper distance.

The new fixed-separation diagnostic finds its sampled upper-Israel root at
\(a=0.09862479074023135\), far inside this analytic no-turn region, with
upper scalar residual \(-0.80144\). The successful long candidate lies above
the threshold and contains the required turnaround. This supports a concrete
reading: the extra separation allows the stabilizing scalar to reverse and
meet the oppositely oriented upper wall condition. It does not yet prove
fixed-separation nonexistence because unobserved tangent or separate branches
remain possible.
