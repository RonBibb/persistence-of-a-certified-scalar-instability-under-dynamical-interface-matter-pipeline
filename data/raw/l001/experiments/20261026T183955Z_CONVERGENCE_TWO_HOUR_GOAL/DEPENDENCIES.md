## Claim boundaries

The construction is a particular local pure-growing solution family, with two amplitude signs. It is not an evolution algorithm for arbitrary data, a full unstable manifold, or an endpoint computation. The signed clock inequality uses proper separation on the chosen homogeneous slicing and the lower wall's proper clock. The oscillator adds a scalar departure witness; it does not make separation itself slicing-independent.

The tiny sufficient radii quantify conservatism in this proof. They neither predict a new physical length/energy scale nor locate a transition. The proof-budget trace share is not a physical force or energy share. A useful-amplitude continuation remains a separate task.
