Keep all prior background, spectral, source-envelope, analytic inverse,

No physical parameter, numerical trajectory, or earlier result is changed.

1. Positive linear response map
The raw load vector is (qT,qM,qK,qR,QI,j,o), with the first four entries
the certified bulk weighted norms and the last three n times the maximum
wall load. Propagate separate nonnegative loads through the same estimates:
 c=Cc qT, xi=Cxi QI,
 Sp=(3/2)c+64qM,
 Sc=18Sp+28c+qR, Sg=2c+6Sp+qK,
 F=Sg+80Sc/(4dc), Rwall=c+(39/4)xi+2j+o/2,
 g=(1/2+1/(4d0))Rwall+Sc/d0,
 Uf=Cscalar(F+g),
 Xp=[Cm(Sc+Uf+Sp)+rho(T Uf/12+Sp/2)]/(1-rho H/2),
 Yp=H Xp+T Uf/6+Sp,
 Up=2Xp+4Yp+(5/2)Uf+9Sp/rho,
 Uchi=Cchi(Uf/2+xi/4+o),
 Y=3Up+c+Uf+2xi+2Uchi.
Here H=3,T=2,Cxi=32768,Cchi=16384, and all other constants are
the authenticated preceding ones. This is a positive linear map of
the seven loads. Evaluate it on each basis vector to get alpha_i>=0.
As a control, sum alpha_i must exactly reproduce the conditioned C
obtained by putting every raw load equal to the same unit bound.

2. Remove only the unused constant and linear envelope coefficients
For each of the seven previously reviewed positive rational envelopes Fi,
compute Mi=Fi(a)-Fi(0)-a Fi'(0) exactly. Every remaining Taylor coefficient
is nonnegative and has degree at least2. Hence f_i,m<=Mi/a^m for m>=2.
The literal rational Fi formulas are unchanged. The program computes
Fi(0),Fi'(0) by exact dual arithmetic and checks against a separately
derived coefficient table. No floating-point subtraction is used.

At order n the inputs are truncated below n, so the constant and linear
parts cannot contribute. The primitive wall harmonic factor is still m;
using the same factor for bulk rows only enlarges the bound. Therefore
the aggregate recurrence is bounded by
 [eta^n] sum_(m>=2) m [sum_i alpha_i f_i,m] Y_<n^m,
and sum_i alpha_i f_i,m<=Mweighted/a^m,
Mweighted=sum_i alpha_i Mi.
Choose K=max(1,1/a,2Mweighted/a²), rounded outward to an integer.
Then (m-1)K^(m-1)>=m Mweighted/a^m at every m>=2, exactly as before.

The directional budget records each alpha_i Mi and its exact fraction
of the total. It identifies which proof estimate limits this construction,
not which physical force dominates the actual nonlinear solution.
The source inventory and all derivative costs are retained. The result
remains a local pure-growing family, not an endpoint or generic-data flow.
