## What is distinct and what is not

The credible distinct object is the explicit, auditable certificate for a nonzero-amplitude prepared datum under one exact numerical and physical contract. Recovering source-mode correlations without changing the datum made a previously failed sufficient inclusion test pass. That is useful proof engineering and closes an actual prerequisite.

The following are not supported novelty claims:

- Inventing interval inclusion, Taylor integration, automatic differentiation, high-precision certification, or a contraction proof.
- Discovering that gravitational boundary data need higher-order compatibility with initial data.
- Inventing nonlinear scalar/two-brane dynamics or its linear wall-mode equations.
- Establishing all-order compatibility, a complete initial-boundary-value theorem, stable evolution, collapse, a dynamical horizon or an event horizon from this five-variable root.
- Claiming a new physical interaction from the shrinking enclosure widths. The certified datum did not change when the enclosure improved.

No exact duplicate was located among the evaluated sources. That is a bounded search outcome, not a “first ever” theorem. A source that gives a compatible-data theorem does not duplicate every later concrete certificate; conversely, a different parameter value does not automatically make a substantial new result.

## Standalone and enabling value

**Standalone:** the current claim is too construction-specific to support a strong standalone novelty pitch on this search alone. Its strongest case is as a reproducible technical result or a component of a broader argument. This is not a prediction that a journal cannot publish it.

A stronger standalone contribution would require a reusable preparation theorem, a demonstrable obstruction and remedy for a meaningful class of data, or a rigorous consequence that follows from the prepared datum and is not already supplied by the literature. Those are possible future directions, not present results.

**Enabling:** the result has direct value now. A missing preparation root is no longer the obstruction in the fixed construction. It can be carried forward as an authenticated premise, preserving the distinction between exact compatibility of a finite formal jet and a solution of the evolution problem. The next useful step is a precise bridge to a faithful initial-data/coordinate representation and an applicable existence or numerical-admission framework, not repeated re-certification of the same passing root.

## Decisions

1. **IMPORT.** Keep the certified artifact. Attribute standard root, integration and jet methods to existing numerical analysis. Use the physical wall/mode framework and recursive compatibility literature with an explicit convention dictionary.
2. **NARROWLY EXTEND.** If further work is authorized, first write a hypothesis-by-hypothesis map from the prepared datum to the chosen next theorem or evolution admission contract. Identify exactly which missing map, regularity, scalar-wall or constraint-propagation estimate must be established. Do not redesign the whole program to chase “first” language.
3. **STOP.** Stop broad novelty framing that the primary sources contradict, and stop rebuilding a generic solver solely to make the existing certificate appear novel. This is not a recommendation to abandon the certified component or to launch more runs.

Bottom line: **covered methods, a narrow incremental fixed-datum result, and meaningful enabling value.** A genuinely new physical mechanism or general theorem remains to be established elsewhere in the research program.
