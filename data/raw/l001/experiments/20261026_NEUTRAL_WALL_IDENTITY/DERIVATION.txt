Exact moving-wall geometric and material residual identity

Purpose: remove the previously explicit but independently unverified wall
Codazzi/material-exchange premise from the neutral formal recursion.
This test does not recompute an ODE or enlarge any convergence radius.

Take the general five-dimensional metric diag(-N²,R²,R²,R²,B²), where
N,R,B depend on t,s and are positive. Let a timelike homogeneous wall be
s=S(t), v=B S_t/N, gamma=(1-v²)^(-1/2), |v|<1. Its unit tangent and
inward oriented normal are U=gamma(N^-1,0,0,0,v/B) and
n=sigma gamma(v/N,0,0,0,B^-1), sigma=+1 or-1. Define
A=N_s/(NB), C=B_t/(NB), D=R_s/(BR), F=R_t/(NR),
along=partial_t+vN/B partial_s, and dot=gamma/N along.
The orientation is factored out of every geometric check.

1. Independent extrinsic geometry
Compute the full five-dimensional Christoffel and Ricci tensors from
the diagonal metric, using arbitrary first and second jets. No bulk
equation or background identity is substituted.
The normal acceleration and spatial extrinsic eigenvalue must give
 Ktau=sigma gamma[gamma² v_t/N+A+vC],
 Kx=sigma gamma[D+vF], Hwall=gamma(F+vD),
where v_t is the total along-wall coordinate derivative.
The acceleration check divides by sigma gamma³, so its result is
 v_t/N+(1-v²)(A+vC). This avoids an assumed square-root simplification.

2. Exact Codazzi component in this ansatz
For K^a_b=diag(Ktau,Kx,Kx,Kx), the temporal proper-time component of
the covariant divergence of K^a_b-K delta^a_b is
 -3 dot Kx+3 Hwall(Ktau-Kx).
Using the expressions above, its v_t contributions cancel exactly by
gamma²(1-v²)=1. Dividing by sigma gamma² leaves
 3[-(along D+v along F)/N+(F+vD)(A-D+v(C-F))].
The independently computed Ricci projection, divided by sigma gamma²,
is v Ric_tt/N²+(1+v²)Ric_ts/(NB)+v Ric_ss/B².
The test compares these as exact Laurent polynomials. This verifies
the Codazzi sign and moving-wall factors for the actual homogeneous
ansatz without using a bulk field equation or sampled numerical data.

3. Material identity, including off-shell residual
For zero current, Vwall=tau(Phi)+W(Phi,chi),
 rho=chi_dot²/2+Vwall, P=chi_dot²/2-Vwall.
Let O=chi_ddot+3Hwall chi_dot+W_chi. Differentiating rho gives
 rho_dot+3Hwall(rho+P)=chi_dot O+Vwall_Phi Phi_dot.
Thus D_a S^a_tau=-chi_dot O-Vwall_Phi Phi_dot.
For the scalar normal residual Jphi=2 nPhi-Vwall_Phi and bulk Einstein
residual E_nU=Ric_nU-(nPhi)(UPhi), with all metric-proportional terms
dropping out by n.U=0, the junction residual
 J^a_b=K^a_b-K delta^a_b+S^a_b/2
satisfies exactly
 D_a J^a_tau=E_nU+(Jphi/2)Phi_dot-(chi_dot/2)O.
This is an off-shell identity. On solved bulk/scalar/oscillator rows
it gives zero divergence. At positive harmonic n the tracefree Israel
row and lower solved wall rows make J_n a pure identity tensor. Its
divergence is lambda_n j_n, lambda_n=nr!=0, hence j_n=0 separately
on each wall. A zero harmonic is not removed by this argument.

4. Exact coordinate checks against the source envelopes
For N=a(S)exp(pwall), R=a(S)exp(ht+pwall), B=exp(bwall),
 ell=N sqrt(1-v²), logarithmic wall derivatives satisfy
 (log R)_t=h+H S_t+pwall_t,
 (log ell)_t=H S_t+pwall_t-v v_t/(1-v²).
Therefore the coordinate oscillator friction 3(log R)_t-(log ell)_t
is 3h+2H S_t+2pwall_t+v v_t/(1-v²), precisely Q5.
For fixed-position derivatives, N_s/N=R_s/R gives A=D, while
C=b_t/N and F=(h+p_t)/N. Consequently Ktau-Kx is exactly S7,
sigma gamma/N[gamma² v_t+v(b_t-h-p_t)].
The scalar normal nPhi=sigma gamma[B^-1 Phi_s+v N^-1 Phi_t]
is the normal row used in Q5 after the consistent pullback.

The polynomial checks qualify this geometry and material exchange for
the neutral homogeneous timelike-wall ansatz. They do not claim a
general junction theorem outside that ansatz, nor generic-data evolution,
or a large-amplitude endpoint. All other action/background/mode premises
remain the already authenticated ones.
