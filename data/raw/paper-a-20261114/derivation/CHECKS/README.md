## What each check establishes

The check IDs C1–C11 and R1–R12 are listed in each script's docstring.

- **check_curvature_jets.py** computes the exact 5D curvature of the perturbed metric ds² = N²ds² + a²(1 + 2εψQ)γ, with N = 1 + εAQ. γ is dS₄, Minkowski or AdS₄ in conformal coordinates. Functions are multivariate truncated Taylor jets with Fraction coefficients, so the curvature is exact at the point. It compares:
  - E_nn, E_sμ, the traceless E_μν, the trace of E_μν and the scalar equation with DERIVATION.md;
  - the radial ADM/Gibbons–Hawking identity;
  - the conformal slice formula;
  - the displaced-wall extrinsic curvature and normal derivative, for both orientations.
- **check_radial_identities.py** computes exact s-derivatives along (9)–(10) with forward-mode dual numbers over Fractions, and exact partial derivatives of the quadratic radial functional ℓ (central differences with unit step are exact for quadratics). It checks:
  - the reductions to (10), 𝒞′ = −4H𝒞 and (M1);
  - the Euler–Lagrange equations of ℓ, and the conjunct identity;
  - the natural boundary conditions;
  - the (FW2) wall algebra and Γ′.
