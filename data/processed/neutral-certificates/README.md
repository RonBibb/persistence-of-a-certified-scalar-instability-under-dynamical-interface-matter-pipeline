Personal local-root, user, compute-host, shared cloud/project and transcript-location strings have been replaced in 35 source/record files by anonymous equivalents. Original and derivative SHA-256 values are both recorded. This changes location metadata and source bytes where such strings occur; it does not create a new historical execution binding. No interval arrays or mathematical predicates were changed. Byte-identical numerical/engine files remain distinguishable from transformed copies through the manifest. All location transformations can be reconciled against originals by the author/editor under an approved access arrangement.

Historical manifests may cover larger packets than this selected package. Historical producer/audit scripts are included for inspection and are **not** the verification entry point above. Some retain environment, runtime-library, predecessor, hashing or lease assumptions. In particular inclusion of the mass contract/freeze record fixes their omission for inspection, not the entire historical runtime. A portable full trajectory regeneration remains a separate qualification task. Shared Arb, equations and inputs do not constitute independent arithmetic.

The supplied verifier checks saved records, not the correctness of the full numerical proof. Acceptance of faithful historical execution, outward Arb arithmetic and the article’s analytical predicates remains explicit. Public deposition, any justified nonpublic-access reason and editor/referee access terms are unresolved author decisions.
